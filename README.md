## Job Interview Simulator
Have you ever had a job interview? Chances are, if you're looking up Ren'Py novels on GitHub, probably not. 
This project gives you a chance to experience a job interview for your dream company - Obliterate Corp. Navigate complicated interview questions to secure a spot as an intern for the evillest company on the block!

# Prerequisites:
- Precalculus: Degrees and Radians
- Physics: Basic physics
- Astronomy: Fundamental lunar knowledge

**Note:** 
The main companies, Obliterate Corp. and Evil Inc., are wholly fictional with zero references, perspective or sentiment toward real-life events or industries.

This project was made as part of HackClub Snowglobe's weekend visual novel challenge.
